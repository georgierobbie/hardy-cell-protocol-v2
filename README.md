Hardy Cell Protocol v2
Frozen measurement of 10,000 consecutive Hardy cells on the critical line.
A Hardy cell is the open interval between two consecutive nontrivial zeros of the Riemann zeta function. Within each cell we measured:
•  the interior transverse breadth of Hardy’s Z-function
•  the normalized location of that maximum
Protocol v2 was sealed before acquisition. Five independent certification gates were applied to every cell. All 10,000 cells passed every gate.
Files
•  Hardy_Cell_Dataset.pdf — full paper (philosophy, protocol, results)
•  Protocol_v2_Specification.pdf — frozen measurement specification
•  hardy_cells_10000.csv — complete certified dataset (33 fields per cell)
No hypothesis testing or interpretation was performed during measurement. The dataset stands alone.
